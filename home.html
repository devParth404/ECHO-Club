01 / ABOUT ECHO

Find Your Voice.

More than a club — a space to communicate, create, collaborate and grow.

What is ECHO?

ECHO — Enhancing Communication, Harmony & Oratory is a student-driven platform dedicated to developing confident speakers, expressive individuals and effective communicators.

ECHO creates opportunities for students to step beyond their comfort zones through public speaking, open mics, interactive activities, discussions, creative expression and leadership experiences.

Here, every voice has a place, every idea deserves to be heard, and every student gets an opportunity to grow.

🎙️ SPEAKBuild confidence and become a powerful, clear and engaging speaker.
💡 EXPRESSShare your ideas, creativity, personality and perspective without hesitation.
🤝 CONNECTBuild meaningful connections through communication, collaboration and community.
